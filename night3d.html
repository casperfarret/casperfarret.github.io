Inside NIGHT

Explore the spectrograph in 3D

NIGHT measures helium escaping from the atmospheres of exoplanets. It takes starlight from a telescope, spreads a narrow slice of near-infrared light around 1083 nm into a very detailed spectrum, and records it on an infrared detector. This is the instrument with its lid off. Rotate the model and select the numbered components to see what each one does.

Guide

Follow the light

The components are numbered in the order the light first reaches them.

On its way from the fibres to the detector the light crosses the bench several times, so some of the mirrors and lenses are used more than once. The red beam is the light on its way to the grating. The orange beam is the same light on its way back to the detector.
