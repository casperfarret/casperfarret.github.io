Instrumentation Projects & Research Experience

2021–present

Project lead, NIGHT (Near-Infrared Gatherer of Helium Transits), University of Geneva

Designed and built a compact, fiber-fed, high-resolution near-infrared spectrograph dedicated to surveying the metastable helium triplet in exoplanet atmospheres. Led an international team of scientists and engineers; collaborated with industrial partners on novel instrumentation for fiber-fed high-resolution spectroscopy, securing >CHF 100,000 in in-kind hardware contributions. First light at the OHP 1.52 m telescope in 2025. Negotiated the installation of NIGHT at the 3.58 m Telescopio Nazionale Galileo (TNG), La Palma.

2025–2026

NUV spectrograph assessment, WALTzER, ESA F-class mission concept

Preliminary assessment of the near-ultraviolet spectrograph: optical design of a toroidal concave-grating spectrograph covering 240–325 nm at R > 2800, with trade studies of the shutter mechanism, dichroic and cross-dispersion sampling.

2021

Project lead, ABORAS, polarimetric Sun-as-a-star telescope for HARPS3

Feb.–Jul. 2020

Research intern, SRON Netherlands Institute for Space Research & TU Delft

Micromachined dielectrics for SPICA/SAFARI: simulation and evaluation of an effective dielectric material for a resonant immersed waveplate.

2017–2021
